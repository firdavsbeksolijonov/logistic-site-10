4.5 Ta'minot liniyalari (Tractor-Trailer ulanishlari) Supply Lines (Tractor-to-Trailer Connections)

Kabina orqasida haydovchi tomonida uchta liniya — harakatlanishdan oldin uchtasini ham ulang:Three lines behind the cab on the driver's side — connect all three before moving:

LiniyaLine RangiColor VazifasiFunction
Favqulodda (ta'minot)Emergency (supply) Qizil (Red)Red Trailer havo tanklarini to'ldiradi, havo yo'qolsa favqulodda tormozlarni qo'yadiFills trailer air tanks, sets emergency brakes if air lost
Xizmat (Service)Service Ko'k (Blue)Blue Tormoz signalini uzatadi — pedalni bosing, trailer tormozlari ishlaydiCarries brake signal — press pedal, trailer brakes apply
Elektr shnuri (7-way)Electrical cord (7-way) Yashil (Green)Green Trailer chiroqlari va ABS ga quvvat beradiPowers trailer lights and ABS

💡 Maslahat: Avval ko'k liniyani ulang — u eng uzoqda joylashgan.💡 Tip: Connect the blue line first — it is the farthest away.

4.6 Avtomobil sigortalar (Fuses) Automotive Fuses

  • Transport vositasi simlarini va jihozlarni himoyalaydi — agar juda ko'p tok oqsa, sigorta zanjirni uzadiProtects vehicle wiring and equipment — if too much current flows, fuse breaks circuit
  • Standart pichoq sigortalar 32V DC gacha baholangan (ba'zilari 42/48V tizimlari uchun yuqoriroq)Standard blade fuses rated up to 32V DC (some higher for 42/48V systems)
  • Kuygan sigortani bir xil amperaj bilan almashtiring, hech qachon yuqoriroq emasReplace blown fuse with same amperage, never higher
  • Zaxira sigortalarni olib yuring — truck avtomatik uzgichlardan foydalanmasa, bu majburiy favqulodda jihozdirCarry spares — required emergency equipment unless truck uses circuit breakers

4.7 DPF Regen (Eng ko'p uchraydigan truck muammosi) DPF Regen (Most Common Truck Issue)

Dizel zarralar filtri (DPF) kuya va kulni tutadi. Kuya davriy ravishda yoqilishi kerak = "regen."The Diesel Particulate Filter (DPF) traps soot and ash. Soot must be burned off periodically = "regen."

Regen turiRegen Type Nima sodir bo'ladiWhat Happens
Passive Uzoq avtomagistral yurishlarida o'z-o'zidan yonib ketadiBurns off by itself on long highway runs
Active Dvigatel haydash paytida avtomatik ravishda issiqlik qo'shadiEngine adds heat automatically while driving
Parked Ogohlantirish chirog'i yonadi — haydovchi to'xtab, boshqaruv panelidan boshlaydi (~20–60 daqiqa)Warning light flashes — driver parks and starts from dash (~20–60 min)

⚠️ Ogohlantirish: Chiroqni e'tiborsiz qoldirsangiz → dvigatel quvvati kamayadi (derate) → ustaxonaga borish kerak bo'ladi.⚠️ Warning: Ignore the light → engine derates (less power) → shop visit required.

Asosiy fikrlar Key Takeaways

  • Trucklar 4 asosiy turga bo'linadi: sleeper truck (uzoq masofali), day cab (mahalliy), expedite straight truck (shoshilinch) va box truck (kichik yuklar). 7 ta yirik brend: Freightliner, Western Star, Kenworth, Peterbilt, Volvo, Mack, International.Trucks come in 4 main types: sleeper (long-haul), day cab (local), expedite straight truck (urgent), and box truck (small loads). 7 major brands: Freightliner, Western Star, Kenworth, Peterbilt, Volvo, Mack, International.
  • Truck tashqi qismlarini bilish pre-trip tekshiruv uchun muhim: old (13 qism), orqa (8 qism) va yon (15 qism) tomondan barcha qismlarni tekshiring.Knowing truck exterior parts is essential for pre-trip inspection: check all components from front (13 parts), rear (8 parts), and side (15 parts) views.
  • Truck olish uchun 3 variant: ijara (rental — qisqa, qimmat), lizing (lease — uzoq, texnik xizmat bilan) va sotib olish lizingi (lease-to-purchase — egalik). Ryder va Penske asosiy provayderlar.3 options for getting a truck: rental (short, expensive), lease (long-term, with maintenance), and lease-to-purchase (own it). Ryder and Penske are major providers.
  • Tractor-trailer orasida 3 ta ta'minot liniyasi: qizil (favqulodda havo), ko'k (tormoz signali) va yashil (elektr). Ko'k liniyani birinchi ulang.3 supply lines connect tractor to trailer: red (emergency air), blue (brake signal), green (electrical). Connect blue first.
  • DPF regen eng ko'p uchraydigan truck muammosi. 3 turi: passive, active, parked. Ogohlantirish chirog'ini e'tiborsiz qoldirmang — dvigatel quvvati pasayadi.DPF regen is the most common truck issue. 3 types: passive, active, parked. Don't ignore the warning light — engine loses power.
