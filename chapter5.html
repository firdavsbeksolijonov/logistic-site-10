5.1 Trailer turlari Types of Trailers

📦 Yopiq (Enclosed) traylerlar📦 Enclosed Trailers

📦

Dry Van

Eng keng tarqalgan — yopiq quti, umumiy yuklarni ob-havo va o'g'rilikdan himoyalaydiMost common — enclosed box, protects general freight from weather and theft

❄️

Reefer

Muzlatgichli trailer — sovuq yoki issiq harorat nazorati kerak bo'lgan yuklar uchunRefrigerated trailer — for loads needing cold or warm temperature control

🏗️ Ochiq (Open/Flatbed) traylerlar🏗️ Open/Flatbed Trailers

🏗️

Flatbed

🚗

Auto Hauler

📐

Step Deck

Double Drop / Lowboy

1️⃣

2️⃣

5.4 Trailer jihozlari Trailer Equipment

🦵 Landing Gear (Tirgovichlar)🦵 Landing Gear

Trailer old qismidagi ikkita oyoq — tractor ulanmagan paytda traylerni ushlab turadi. Tractor bilan ulanganda oyoqlar ko'tariladi (crank yordamida). Ulanishdan oldin oyoqlarni tushirish va yukdan keyin ko'tarish kerak.Two legs at front of trailer — supports trailer when no tractor attached. Raised (via crank) when connected to tractor. Lower before unhooking, raise after coupling.

🔗 Fifth Wheel (Beshinchi g'ildirak)🔗 Fifth Wheel

Tractor orqasidagi ulanish plitasi. Trailer kingpin-ni qulflaydi.Coupling plate on back of tractor. Locks onto trailer kingpin.

Pre-trip tekshiruv (4 nuqta):Pre-trip check (4 points):

  • Kingpin to'g'ri qulflangan — tug test o'tkazingKingpin properly locked — do a tug test
  • Release handle yopiq va qulflanganRelease handle closed and locked
  • Tirqish yo'q — plita va trailer orasida bo'shliq tekshiringNo gap — check space between plate and trailer
  • Moylash — fifth wheel yuza greased bo'lishi kerakLubrication — fifth wheel surface must be greased

Sliding fifth wheel: Og'irlikni qayta taqsimlash uchun oldga/orqaga siljitiladi — steer o'qi og'irligini moslash uchun ishlatiladi.Sliding fifth wheel: Moves forward/back to redistribute weight — used to adjust steer axle weight.

🛑 Tormoz vanalari (Brake Valves)🛑 Brake Valves

🔶 Sariq olmos (Yellow Diamond)🔶 Yellow Diamond

Parking/Emergency tormoz. Tortib chiqarish = tormoz qo'yiladi. Bosish = tormoz bo'shatiladi. Havo yo'qolsa avtomatik tormozlaydi.Parking/Emergency brake. Pull out = brakes set. Push in = brakes released. Auto-applies if air lost.

🔴 Qizil sakkizburchak (Red Octagon)🔴 Red Octagon

Trailer tormoz nazorati. Tortib chiqarish = faqat trailer tormozlari qo'yiladi. Tekshiruv va bobtail haydash uchun ishlatiladi.Trailer brake control. Pull out = only trailer brakes apply. Used for testing and bobtail driving.

5.5 Og'irlik balansi Weight Balance

Federal og'irlik chegaralariFederal Weight Limits

O'q / JamiAxle / Total Maksimal og'irlikMax Weight
GVW (Jami yuk og'irligi)GVW (Gross Vehicle Weight)80,000 lbs
Steer o'qiSteer axle~12,000 lbs
Drive o'qlariDrive axles34,000 lbs
Trailer o'qlariTrailer axles34,000 lbs

⚖️ CAT Scale⚖️ CAT Scale

  • Truck stop-lardagi sertifikatlangan tarozilar — o'q bo'yicha og'irlikni ko'rsatadiCertified scales at truck stops — shows weight per axle group
  • Birinchi tortish ~$12–15, qayta tortish bepul (bir xil joyda)First weigh ~$12–15, reweigh free (same location)
  • Chipta (ticket) steer, drive va trailer o'qlarini alohida ko'rsatadiTicket shows steer, drive, and trailer axles separately
  • Haydovchi truckni taroziga to'g'ri joylashtirishi kerak — bo'limlarga to'g'ri to'xtash muhimDriver must position truck correctly on scale — proper stop on sections matters
  • CAT Scale kafolati: Agar chiptadagi og'irlik noto'g'ri bo'lsa va jarima olinsa, CAT Scale jarimani qoplaydiCAT Scale guarantee: If ticket weight is wrong and you get fined, CAT Scale covers the fine

⚠️ Ortiqcha og'irlik bo'lsa (3 qadam)⚠️ If Overweight (3 steps)

  1. Sliding fifth wheel: Fifth wheel-ni oldga/orqaga siljiting — steer va drive orasida og'irlikni qayta taqsimlaydiSliding fifth wheel: Slide fifth wheel forward/back — redistributes weight between steer and drive
  2. Sliding tandem: Trailer tandem o'qlarini oldga/orqaga siljiting — drive va trailer orasida og'irlikni o'zgartiradiSliding tandem: Slide trailer tandem axles forward/back — shifts weight between drive and trailer
  3. Yukni qayta joylashtirish: Agar sliding yetarli bo'lmasa, yukni trailer ichida qayta joylashtiringReload cargo: If sliding isn't enough, reposition cargo inside trailer

5.6 Avtopark operatsiyalari Fleet Operations

1️⃣

Carrier o'z traylerlarini boshqaradi yoki trailer pool/interchangedan foydalanadiCarrier manages own trailers or uses trailer pool/interchange

2️⃣

Traylerni dock-da tashlab ketish (drop-and-hook) — haydovchi kutmaydi, vaqt tejaydiDropping trailer at dock (drop-and-hook) — driver doesn't wait, saves time

3️⃣

Trailer tracking — GPS va ELD orqali traylerni real vaqtda kuzatishTrailer tracking — real-time monitoring via GPS and ELD

4️⃣

Trailer texnik xizmat jadvali — shinalar, tormozlar, chiroqlar, devor holatiTrailer maintenance schedule — tires, brakes, lights, wall condition

5️⃣

Trailer inspektsiyasi — FMCSA yillik inspektsiya (DOT stikeri) talab qilinadiTrailer inspection — FMCSA annual inspection (DOT sticker) required

6️⃣

Pre-trip va post-trip — har safar haydovchi traylerni tekshirishi kerakPre-trip and post-trip — driver must inspect trailer every time

7️⃣

Trailer ijarasi va pooling — kichik carrierlar ko'pincha traylerni ijaraga oladiTrailer rental and pooling — smaller carriers often rent trailers

Asosiy fikrlar Key Takeaways

  • Traylerlar 5 guruhda 15 turga bo'linadi: yopiq (dry van, reefer), ochiq (flatbed, auto hauler, step deck, lowboy), maxsus (dump, pneumatic, hopper, tanker, livestock, conestoga) va boshqalar (container chassis, carnival ride, frac tank).Trailers come in 15 types across 5 groups: enclosed (dry van, reefer), open (flatbed, auto hauler, step deck, lowboy), specialty (dump, pneumatic, hopper, tanker, livestock, conestoga), and others (container chassis, carnival ride, frac tank).
  • Standart 53 ft dry van 26 palet sig'adi. Eng ko'p ishlatiladigan konfiguratsiya: swing doors, E-track, yog'och pol. Reefer harorat diapazoni: -20°F dan +80°F gacha.A standard 53 ft dry van holds 26 pallets. Most common setup: swing doors, E-track, wooden floor. Reefer range: -20°F to +80°F.
  • Asosiy trailer jihozlari: landing gear (tirgovichlar — tractor bo'lmaganda tiraydi), fifth wheel (ulanish plitasi — 4 nuqtali pre-trip tekshiruv), va tormoz vanalari (sariq olmos = parking, qizil sakkizburchak = trailer tormoz).Key trailer equipment: landing gear (supports without tractor), fifth wheel (coupling plate — 4-point pre-trip check), and brake valves (yellow diamond = parking, red octagon = trailer brake).
  • Federal og'irlik chegarasi: GVW 80,000 lbs, steer ~12,000, drive 34,000, trailer 34,000. CAT Scale da tortish ~$12–15. Ortiqcha og'irlikda: fifth wheel siljiting → tandem siljiting → yukni qayta joylashtiring.Federal weight limit: GVW 80,000 lbs, steer ~12,000, drive 34,000, trailer 34,000. CAT Scale weigh ~$12–15. If overweight: slide fifth wheel → slide tandem → reload cargo.
