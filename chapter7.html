Yuk xavfsizligi va protseduralar Load Security and Procedures

17 slayd  ·  Yukni mahkamlash, asboblar, tiedown qoidalari, yuklash jihozlari, doklar, ob'ekt turlari, yuklash/tushirish tartibi 17 slides  ·  Securing loads, tools, tiedown rules, loading equipment, docks, facility types, loading/unloading procedures

7.1 Nega yukni mahkamlash kerak? Why Secure the Load?

  • Siljigan, ag'darigan yoki tushib ketgan yuk tovarni, trailerni va boshqa haydovchilarni zararlaydi Cargo that shifts, tips, or falls damages freight, trailer, and other drivers
  • Federal qoidalar: 49 CFR 393.100–136 (FMCSA yuk mahkamlash standartlari) Federal rules: 49 CFR 393.100–136 (FMCSA cargo securement)
  • Haydovchi birinchi 50 mil ichida yukni tekshirishi kerak, keyin har 150 mil yoki 3 soatda Driver checks load within first 50 miles, then every 150 miles or 3 hours
  • Plomblangan yuklar haydovchi ocholmaydigan bo'lsa, tekshiruvdan mustasno Sealed loads driver can't open are exempt from those checks
  • Shipper yuklaydi, carrier tashiydi — zarar da'volari ko'pincha kim yuklaganiga bog'liq Shipper loads, carrier hauls — damage claims often depend on who loaded

7.2 Mahkamlash asboblari Securing Tools

1. Yuk tasmalari (Load Straps) 1. Load Straps

  • Yukni pastga yoki joyida ushlab turadigan to'qilgan tasmalar Webbing straps that hold freight down or in place
  • Vanda: devordagi e-track ga qistiriladi In van: clip into e-track on walls
  • Flatbedda: rub railga ilintiradi, ratchet yoki winch bilan tortadi On flatbed: hook to rub rail, pull tight with ratchet or winch

2. Yuk barlari (Load Bars / Load Locks) 2. Load Bars (Load Locks)

  • Sozlanadigan barlar — trailer ichida yukning orqasiga o'rnatiladi, tormozlashda siljishni oldini oladi Adjustable bars set across trailer behind freight — prevents sliding when braking
  • E-track barlari: Uchki qismlari e-track relslariga qulflanadi E-track bars: End fittings lock into e-track rails
  • Ishqalanish barlari: Yassi rezina uchlari ratchet orqali devorlarga bosiladi — har qanday trailerda ishlaydi Friction bars: Flat rubber ends press against walls by ratchet — work in any trailer

3. Block va Brace, Qulflar va Plombalar 3. Block and Brace, Locks and Seals

  • Block and brace: Yog'och yukni atrofiga mixlanadi/ponalanadi — siljishni to'xtatadi Block and brace: Lumber nailed/wedged around freight to stop sliding
  • Padlocks: O'g'irlikni to'xtatadi Padlocks: Stop theft
  • Raqamlangan plombalar (Numbered seals): Eshiklar ochilmaganini isbotlaydi — plomba raqamini BOL ga yozing Numbered seals: Prove doors weren't opened — write seal number on BOL

4. Zanjirlar, Binder va Dunnage Airbag 4. Chains, Binders, and Dunnage Airbags

  • Zanjirlar va binderlar: Og'ir flatbed yuklari uchun — po'lat rulonlar, mashinalar, quvurlar. Binder (ratchet/lever) zanjirni tortib mahkamlaydi Chains and binders: For heavy flatbed freight — steel coils, machinery, pipe. Binder (ratchet/lever) pulls chain tight
  • Dunnage airbaglar: Palletlar orasidagi bo'shliqlarda shishiriladi — siljishni oldini oladi Dunnage airbags: Inflated in gaps between pallets — prevents shifting

5. Kingpin Lock va Adyollar 5. Kingpin Lock and Blankets

  • Kingpin lock: Kingpin ustiga qulflanadigan quyma po'lat qopqoq — trailer o'g'irligini oldini oladi Kingpin lock: Cast-steel cover locked over kingpin — prevents trailer theft
  • Adyollar (Blankets): Mebel/tayyor mahsulotlarni tirnalishdan himoya qiladi — blanket-wrap yuklari uchun ishlatiladi Blankets: Protect furniture/finished goods from scratches — used for blanket-wrap loads

7.3 Ochiq platformali tiedown qoidalari Open Deck Tiedown Rules

Ish yuki chegarasi (Working Load Limit) ≥ yuk og'irligining 50%iga teng bo'lishi kerak. Working load limits of tiedowns must be ≥ 50% of cargo weight.

Yuk o'lchami Cargo Size Tiedown soni Tiedowns Required
≤5 ft va ≤1,100 lb ≤5 ft and ≤1,100 lb 1
≤5 ft va og'irroq, yoki 5–10 ft ≤5 ft and heavier, or 5–10 ft 2
>10 ft >10 ft 2 + har qo'shimcha 10 ft uchun 1 ta 2 + 1 for every extra 10 ft

⚠️ G'altaklar (coils), yog'ochlar (logs), mashinalar (machinery) va transport vositalari uchun qo'shimcha maxsus qoidalar mavjud. ⚠️ Coils, logs, machinery, and vehicles have extra specific rules beyond the general table.

Jihoz Equipment Tavsif Description

7.5 Yuklash doklari Loading Docks

Binoning trucklar orqaga kiradigan qismi — odatda trailer pol balandligida (~4 ft) Part of building where trucks back in — usually at trailer floor height (~4 ft)

  • Dok leveler: Dok va trailer poli orasidagi bo'shliqni ko'prik qiluvchi plastina Dock leveler: Plate bridging gap to trailer floor
  • Muhrlash/boshpana (Seal/shelter): Eshik atrofidagi yostiqchalar — ob-havoni tashqarida ushlab turadi Seal/shelter: Pads around door keeping weather out
  • Vehicle restraint (Dock lock): Orqa bamperni ilmoqlaydi — trailer yurmasligi uchun Vehicle restraint (dock lock): Hooks rear bumper so trailer can't roll away
  • Wheel chocks: Dok lock bo'lmaganda g'ildiraklar orqasiga qo'yiladigan bloklar Wheel chocks: Blocks behind tires where no dock lock

Asosiy fikrlar Key Takeaways

  • Federal qoidalar (49 CFR 393.100–136) yukni mahkamlashni tartibga soladi. Haydovchi birinchi 50 mil ichida, keyin har 150 mil yoki 3 soatda tekshirishi shart. Federal rules (49 CFR 393.100–136) govern cargo securement. Driver must check within the first 50 miles, then every 150 miles or 3 hours.
  • Asosiy mahkamlash asboblari: tasmalar (straps), yuk barlari (load bars), zanjirlar (chains), binderlar, dunnage airbaglar, plombalar va qulflar. Main securing tools: straps, load bars, chains, binders, dunnage airbags, seals and locks.
  • Ochiq platformada (flatbed) tiedown ish yuki chegarasi yuk og'irligining kamida 50% bo'lishi kerak. 10 ft dan uzun yuklar uchun har 10 ft ga qo'shimcha tiedown kerak. On open decks (flatbed), tiedown working load limits must be at least 50% of cargo weight. Loads over 10 ft need one extra tiedown per 10 ft.
  • Yuklash jihozlari: forklift (OSHA treningi kerak), pallet jack, elektr pallet jack va yard jockey. Loading equipment: forklift (needs OSHA training), pallet jack, electric pallet jack and yard jockey.
  • Yuklash usullari: Live load (haydovchi kutadi), Drop trailer (qoldiriladi, 24–72 soat), Hook and drop (tayyor trailerni ulash). Bepul vaqtdan (odatda 2 soat) keyin detention to'lovi hisoblanadi. Loading methods: Live load (driver waits), Drop trailer (left for 24–72 hours), Hook and drop (hook pre-loaded trailer). Detention charges apply after free time (usually 2 hours).
