6.4 Umumiy yuk klassifikatsiyasi General Freight Classification

  • 📦
    Palletlangan (Palletized) Palletized

    Har bir pallet = bitta dona, birga qoladi. Tez yuklash, kamroq zarar Each pallet = one piece, stays together. Fast loading, less damage

  • 🧱
    Polga yuklangan (Floor loaded) Floor loaded

    Qutilar qo'lda terilib chiqiladi — ko'proq sig'adi, lekin yuklash/tushirish soatlab davom etadi Boxes stacked by hand — holds more, but loading/unloading takes hours

  • 🪵
    Qutilanagan (Crated) Crated

    Og'ir/nozik buyumlar yog'och qutilarda, forklift bilan ko'tariladi Heavy/delicate items in wooden crates, handled by forklift

6.7 Xavfli yuklarlar (Hazmat) Hazardous Freight (Hazmat)

  • 49 CFR 171–180 qoidalari bilan boshqariladi Governed by 49 CFR Parts 171–180
  • Part 172: Xavfli materiallar jadvali, jo'natish hujjatlari, belgilar, yorliqlar, plakardlar, trening, xavfsizlik Part 172: Hazardous Materials Table, shipping papers, marking, labels, placards, training, security
  • Eng xavfli materiallar uchun har qanday miqdorda plakardlar kerak; boshqalar uchun 1,001 lb dan Placards needed for any amount of most dangerous materials; from 1,001 lb for others
  • UN raqamlari: 4 raqamli ID (UN 1203 = benzin) UN numbers: 4-digit IDs (UN 1203 = gasoline)
  • Haydovchiga H endorsement + TSA tekshiruvi kerak Driver needs H endorsement + TSA background check
  • Carrierga yuqoriroq sug'urta kerak ($1M–$5M) Carrier needs higher insurance ($1M–$5M)

6.8 Intermodal yuklarlar Intermodal Freight

Bitta konteyner kema → temir yo'l → truck orqali yuk tegmasdan harakatlanadi. Kamroq ko'chirish = kamroq zarar/yo'qotish; uzoq masofalar uchun yo'l transportidan arzonroq. One container moves by ship → rail → truck without freight being touched. Less handling = less damage/loss; long distances cost less than over-the-road.

6.10 Power Only yuklar Power Only Loads

Trailer allaqachon yuklangan, shipper/brokerga tegishli. Carrier faqat tractor ("power") olib kelib ulaydi va tortadi. Trailer already loaded, belongs to shipper/broker. Carrier brings only the tractor ("power") to hook up and haul.

Asosiy tushunchalar: Key terms:

  • Bobtail — Trailersiz tractor haydash Bobtail — Driving tractor with no trailer
  • Trailer interchange insurance — Boshqa birovning traylerini tortish uchun majburiy sug'urta Trailer interchange insurance — Required — hauling someone else's trailer

Keng tarqalgan Power Only yuklar: Common Power Only Loads:

  • Yuklangan dry van, yuklangan reefer, yuklangan flatbed Loaded dry van, loaded reefer, loaded flatbed
  • Juft traylerlar, intermodal konteyner Double trailers, intermodal container
  • Chassis to'plami, frac tank, attraksion Stack of chassis, frac tank, amusement ride

6.11 Deadhead (bo'sh yurish) Deadhead

Bo'sh trailer bilan haydash — yuk yo'q, to'lov yo'q. Yetkazishdan keyin, keyingi yuklash joyiga/truck stopga/uyga yo'lda sodir bo'ladi. Driving with empty trailer — no load, no pay. Happens after delivery, on way to next pickup/truck stop/home.

📊 Dispatcherning hisobi: 📊 Dispatcher's math:

Mil boshiga narx = yuk to'lovi ÷ (yukli millar + deadhead millar) Rate per mile = load pay ÷ (loaded miles + deadhead miles)

  • $2,000 / 800 yukli mil = $2.50/mi $2,000 for 800 loaded miles = $2.50/mi
  • 200 deadhead mil bilan = $2.00/mi (haqiqiy narx) With 200 deadhead miles = $2.00/mi (real rate)

6.12 Juft va uchlik traylerlar (Pups) Doubles and Triples (Pups)

  • Ikki yoki uchta qisqa 28 ft traylerlar ("pups") converter dolly bilan birga tortiladi Two or three short 28 ft trailers ("pups") pulled together with converter dolly
  • Asosan UPS, FedEx, USPS, Amazon uchun ishlatiladi — ko'pincha LTL linehaul sifatida Run mostly for UPS, FedEx, USPS, Amazon — often as LTL linehaul
  • Har bir pup turli manzilga ajratilishi mumkin Each pup can split off to different destination
  • Uchliklar faqat ba'zi (asosan g'arbiy) shtatlarda qonuniy Triples legal only in some (mostly western) states
  • CDL da T endorsement kerak Requires T endorsement on CDL

6.13 Tezkor va yuqori qiymatli yuklar Expedited and High-Value Loads

  • Vaqt jihatdan muhim yuk: to'xtatilgan yig'ish liniyasi uchun avto qismlari, tibbiy buyumlar, bayram posilkalari Time-critical freight: auto parts for stopped assembly line, medical supplies, holiday parcels
  • Cargo van, straight truck yoki semi bilan tashiladi — eng yaqini qaysi bo'lsa Moved by cargo van, straight truck, or semi — whatever is closest
  • Uzoq masofalar uchun jamoaviy haydovchilar ishlatiladi Long runs use team drivers
  • Yuqori qiymatli yuklar: qo'shimcha xavfsizlik — jo'natuvchi yaqinida to'xtamaslik, qulflangan trailer, kuzatuv High-value loads: extra security — no stops near shipper, locked trailer, tracking
  • "Straight thru": keraksiz to'xtashlarsiz yetkaziladi "Straight thru": Delivered with no unnecessary stops

6.15 Doimiy yuklar va marshrutlar Dedicated Loads and Lanes

Bir yoki bir necha truck shartnoma asosida belgilangan jadval bilan bir xil marshrut (A→B) bo'ylab yurishadi. One or more trucks run same route (A→B) on contract with fixed schedule.

Afzalliklari: Pros:

  • Bozordan qat'i nazar barqaror daromad, trucklar har kuni band Steady gross regardless of market, trucks covered every day
  • Mijoz/broker bilan yuqori reyting High rating with customer/broker

Kamchiliklari: Cons:

  • Ba'zan yetarli truck yo'q, qaytish yo'nalishi uchun yuk kerak Sometimes not enough trucks, need backhaul if not round trip
  • Yuqori mavsumda qoplash qiyin Hard to cover in peak season

Dispatcher vazifasi: Brokerlar va shipperlar bilan kuchli aloqalar o'rnatish va doimiy marshrutlarni yutib olish. Dispatcher's job: Build strong relationships with brokers and shippers to win dedicated lanes.

Asosiy fikrlar Key Takeaways

  • Yuk uch xil hajmda tashiladi: LTL (kichik yuklar terminallar orqali), Partial (o'rtacha, terminalsiz) va Truckload (butun trailer). Har birining narxlash usuli farq qiladi. Freight moves in three sizes: LTL (small shipments through terminals), Partial (mid-size, no terminals) and Truckload (whole trailer). Each has different pricing.
  • Xavfli yuk (Hazmat) 49 CFR 171–180 bilan boshqariladi va 9 ta xavf klassiga bo'linadi. Haydovchiga H endorsement va TSA tekshiruvi, carrierga yuqoriroq sug'urta kerak. Hazardous freight (Hazmat) is governed by 49 CFR 171–180 and divided into 9 hazard classes. Driver needs H endorsement and TSA check, carrier needs higher insurance.
  • Intermodal yuk kema → temir yo'l → truck orqali konteyner tegmasdan harakatlanadi. Uzoq masofalar uchun arzonroq va kamroq zarar. Intermodal freight moves by ship → rail → truck without touching the container. Cheaper for long distances with less damage.
  • Power Only da carrier faqat tractor olib keladi — trailer allaqachon yuklangan. Bobtail — trailersiz tractor haydash. In Power Only, carrier brings only the tractor — trailer is already loaded. Bobtail means driving tractor with no trailer.
  • Deadhead (bo'sh yurish) haqiqiy mil boshiga narxni pasaytiradi. Dispatcher hisobi: $2,000 / (800 yukli + 200 bo'sh) = $2.00/mi. Deadhead (empty miles) lowers the real rate per mile. Dispatcher's math: $2,000 / (800 loaded + 200 empty) = $2.00/mi.
  • Doimiy marshrutlar (Dedicated lanes) bozordan qat'i nazar barqaror daromad beradi. Dispatcherning vazifasi — brokerlar bilan kuchli munosabat o'rnatish. Dedicated lanes provide steady income regardless of market. Dispatcher's job — build strong relationships with brokers.
