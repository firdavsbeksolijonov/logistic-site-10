2.1 Uchta asosiy ishtirokchi Three Main Stakeholders

Ishtirokchi Stakeholder Vazifasi Role
Customer (Shipper/Receiver) Tashishga muhtoj bo'lgan yukka egalik qiluvchi yoki manfaatdor tomon Owns or is interested in freight that needs transportation
Broker Shipper va carrier o'rtasidagi vositachi; logistika va tashish jarayonini boshqaradi Intermediary between shipper and carrier; manages logistics and transportation process
Carrier Yukni A nuqtadan B nuqtaga tashish uchun tegishli jihozlarga (trucklarga) ega kompaniya Asset-based company with proper equipment (trucks) to move freight from point A to point B

2.3 Freight Forwarder Freight Forwarder

  • Sotuvchi yoki xaridor nomidan tovar tashishni tashkil qiluvchi shaxs yoki korporatsiya A person or corporation who arranges transport of goods on behalf of either the seller or buyer
  • Ko'pincha bir necha kichik yukni bitta kattaroq yukga birlashtiradi — yaxshiroq yuk narxlari uchun Often consolidates several small shipments into one larger one for better freight rates
  • Ko'p hollarda carrier sifatida harakat qilishning huquqiy mas'uliyatini o'z zimmasiga oladi In most cases assumes legal liabilities of acting as a carrier
  • Odatda multimodal yuklarni tashish bilan shug'ullanadi Usually works on multimodal shipments

2.4 Deal Flow — $3,000 yuk qanday ishlaydi The Deal Flow — How a $3,000 Load Works

Jarayon bosqichlari: Process steps:

  • 1. Shipper yukni e'lon qiladi 1. Shipper posts freight
  • 2. Broker yukni $3,000 ga oladi 2. Broker books it for $3,000
  • 3. Broker carrierga $2,200 taklif qiladi 3. Broker offers carrier $2,200
  • 4. Carrier qabul qiladi (+$2,200) 4. Carrier accepts (+$2,200)
  • 5. Broker $800 marjani saqlaydi (+$800) 5. Broker keeps $800 margin (+$800)
  • 6. Rate Confirmation carrierga yuboriladi 6. Rate Confirmation sent to carrier
  • 7. Haydovchi yukni oladi → Bill of Lading (BOL) imzolanadi 7. Driver picks up → Bill of Lading (BOL) signed
  • 8. Haydovchi yetkazadi → BOL qabul qiluvchida imzolanadi 8. Driver delivers → BOL signed at receiver
  • 9. Carrier brokerga hisob-faktura yuboradi → Broker shipperga hisob-faktura yuboradi 9. Carrier invoices broker → Broker invoices shipper

2.5 Zarar holati jarayoni Damage Case Flow

Zarar aniqlanganda: When damage is found:

  • 1. Xuddi shu deal flow, lekin yetkazishda: 1. Same deal flow, but at delivery:
  • 2. Qabul qiluvchi BOLda zararni qayd etadi → DAMAGE NOTE 2. Receiver notes damage on BOL → DAMAGE NOTE
  • 3. Broker carrierga $2,200 to'lovni USHLAB TURADI 3. Broker HOLDS $2,200 payment to carrier
  • 4. Tergov boshlanadi 4. Investigation begins

Yuk zarari oqibatlari: Consequences of Load Damage:

⚠️ Broker bilan munosabat yomonlashadi Bad relationship with broker
🚫 "No buy" — carrier o'sha mijoz uchun qora ro'yxatga tushadi "No buy" — carrier blacklisted for that customer
📋 Sug'urtaga da'vo (claim) Claim on insurance
💰 Sug'urta narxi oshadi Increase of insurance cost
⏳ Buxgalteriya tomonida kechikish Delay on accounting side
🕐 Yechim topish uzoq davom etadi Long solution time

Dispatcherning vazifasi: Dispatcher's role:

Butun tergov jarayonini nazorat qilish; noto'g'ri boshqarilsa kompaniya obro'siga zarar yetkaziladi Control the whole investigation process; damage to company reputation if mishandled

Asosiy fikrlar Key Takeaways

  • Yuk tashish sanoatida uchta asosiy ishtirokchi bor: Customer (Shipper/Receiver), Broker va Carrier. Har birining o'z vazifasi bor. There are three main stakeholders in trucking: Customer (Shipper/Receiver), Broker, and Carrier. Each has a distinct role.
  • Trucking Company bir necha trucklarga ega, Owner Operator esa o'z truckida mustaqil ishlaydi. Freight Forwarder sotuvchi/xaridor nomidan tashishni tashkil qiladi. A Trucking Company has multiple trucks, while an Owner Operator works independently with their own truck. A Freight Forwarder arranges transport on behalf of sellers/buyers.
  • $3,000 yukda broker $800 marjani saqlaydi, carrier $2,200 oladi. Jarayon Rate Confirmation va BOL hujjatlari orqali rasmiylashtiriladi. On a $3,000 load, the broker keeps $800 margin, carrier receives $2,200. The process is formalized through Rate Confirmation and BOL documents.
  • Yuk zarari jiddiy oqibatlarga olib keladi: broker bilan munosabat buzilishi, qora ro'yxat, sug'urta xarajatlari oshishi. Dispatcher butun tergov jarayonini nazorat qiladi. Load damage has serious consequences: broken broker relationship, blacklisting, increased insurance costs. The dispatcher controls the entire investigation process.
