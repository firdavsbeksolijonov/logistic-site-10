AQSh avtomagistral tizimi US Highway and Road System

24 slayd  ·  AQSh mintaqalari, vaqt zonalari, interstate va intrastate, avtomagistral raqamlash, marshrutlash, pullik yo'llar 24 slides  ·  US regions, time zones, interstate vs intrastate, highway numbering, routing issues, toll roads

8.1 AQSh mintaqalari US Regions

Mintaqa Region Shtatlar States
Northeast New England, NY, NJ, PA New England, NY, NJ, PA
Midwest Ohio dan Dakotalar va Kanzasgacha From Ohio to the Dakotas and Kansas
South Delaware va Floridadan Texasgacha From Delaware and Florida to Texas
West Tog'li mintaqalar, Janubi-g'arb va Tinch okeani sohili The Rockies, the Southwest, and the Pacific coast

Xaritani mintaqalarga bo'lish dispetcherga marshrutlarni guruhlash, narxlarni kuzatish va truckning keyingi yo'nalishini rejalashtirish imkonini beradi. Splitting the map into regions helps a dispatcher group lanes, follow rates, and plan where a truck goes next.

  • Mart — Noyabr: ko'pchilik shtatlar yozgi vaqtdan foydalanadi (EDT, CDT, MDT, PDT) March to November: most states use daylight time (EDT, CDT, MDT, PDT)

8.3 Interstate va Intrastate Interstate vs Intrastate

Tur Type Tavsif Description
Intrastate Tashuvchi faqat bitta shtat ichida yuk tashiydi; yuk uzoqroq shtatlararo sayohatning bir qismi emas Carrier hauls only inside one state; freight not part of longer interstate trip
Interstate Yuk bir shtatdan boshqasiga boradi yoki ichki sayohat shtatlararo/xalqaro jo'natmaning bir qismi Freight goes from one state to another, or intrastate trip is part of interstate/international shipment
  • Bu CDL endorsement emas — har bir CDL egasi haydash turini o'zi tasdiqlaydi Not a CDL endorsement — every CDL holder self-certifies driving type
  • Interstate haydovchilar: 21 yoshdan katta bo'lishi va DOT tibbiy kartasini olib yurishi kerak Interstate drivers: must be 21+, carry DOT medical card
  • Tashuvchiga ko'pchilik yollanma yuklar uchun USDOT raqami + MC raqami kerak Carrier needs USDOT number + MC number for most for-hire loads

8.4 Avtomagistral tizimi — Uch tur Highway System — Three Types

Tur Type Tavsif Description Belgi Sign
Interstate highways ~49,000 mil bo'lingan yo'l, svetoforsiz, faqat kirish yo'llari orqali ~49,000 miles of divided freeway, no traffic lights, ramp entry only Qizil/oq/ko'k qalqon Red/white/blue shield
US highways Eski milliy tarmoq (1926-yildan), ko'pincha shaharchalar orqali 2-qatorli Older national network (since 1926), often 2-lane through towns Oq qalqon White shield
State highways Har bir shtat tomonidan qurilgan/raqamlangan, belgi shakli har xil, sifati turlicha Built/numbered by each state, sign shape varies, quality ranges Shtatga xos State-specific

8.5 Interstate avtomagistral raqamlash Interstate Highway Numbering

  • Toq raqamlar: Shimol–janub (g'arbda past: I-5; sharqda baland: I-95) Odd numbers: North–south (low in west: I-5; high in east: I-95)
  • Juft raqamlar: Sharq–g'arb (janubda past: I-10; shimolda baland: I-90, I-94) Even numbers: East–west (low in south: I-10; high in north: I-90, I-94)
  • Asosiy mamlakat bo'ylab yo'llar: 0 yoki 5 bilan tugaydi (I-10, I-40, I-80, I-35, I-75) Main cross-country routes: End in 0 or 5 (I-10, I-40, I-80, I-35, I-75)
  • Uch raqamli: Juft birinchi raqam = asosiy yo'lga qaytadigan halqa (I-495); toq birinchi raqam = shaharga kiradigan tarmog'i Three digits: Even first digit = loop that rejoins main road (I-495); odd first digit = spur into city

8.6 Chiqishlar, mil belgilari va yo'nalish Exits, Mile Markers, and Direction

  • Chiqishlar (Exits): Interstate dan chiqishning yagona yo'li — yoqilg'i, ovqat, kesishuvchi yo'llarga kirish yo'llari orqali Exits: Only way off interstate — fuel, food, crossing roads reached by ramps
  • Mil belgilari: Janubiy/g'arbiy shtat chegarasida 0 dan boshlanib, yuqoriga sanaydi. Politsiya/tortish/ta'mirlashga truck qayerdaligini aytadi Mile markers: Start at 0 at south/west state line, count up. Tell police/towing/repair where truck is
  • Chiqish raqamlari eng yaqin mil belgisiga mos keladi (Exit 44 ≈ mile 44) Exit numbers match nearest mile marker (Exit 44 ≈ mile 44)
  • Yo'nalish: Toq marshrutlarda shimol/janubga, juft marshrutlarda sharq/g'arbga Direction: Northbound/southbound on odd routes, eastbound/westbound on even

8.7 US va shtat avtomagistrallari US and State Highways

  • US avtomagistrallari raqamlashi Interstate ga teskari: toq S-J marshrutlari sharqda boshlanadi (US 1) va g'arbda tugaydi (US 101) US highways numbering is reverse of interstates: odd N-S routes start in east (US 1) and end in west (US 101)
  • Shtat avtomagistrallari: har bir shtatning o'z tizimi/belgisi bor; band bo'lganlari Interstate standartida qurilgan, tinch joylardagi tor va og'irlik chegarasi past bo'lishi mumkin State highways: every state has own system/sign; busy ones built to Interstate standard, quiet ones can be narrow with low weight limits

8.8 Marshrutlash muammolari Routing Issues

Xarita muammolari Map Issues

  • Tog'lar: Rokiy tog'lari, Sierra Nevada, Appalachi — sekin; kuz-bahor yopilishi yoki zanjir talab qilishi mumkin Mountains: Rockies, Sierra Nevada, Appalachians — slow; fall-spring may close or need chains
  • Ob-havo: Qor, shamol, bo'ronlar truckni soatlab/kunlab to'xtatishi mumkin Weather: Snow, wind, storms can stop truck for hours/days
  • Katta shaharlar: NYC, Chicago, LA, Atlantada pik soatlar soatlab vaqt oladi Big cities: Rush hour in NYC, Chicago, LA, Atlanta costs hours

Avtomobil xaritalari trucklarni noto'g'ri yo'naltiradi Car Maps Send Trucks Wrong Way

  • Kanada orqali: Shimoli-sharqdan Midwest ga Ontario orqali kesib o'tishi mumkin — Kanada ruxsati va pasportsiz, Erie ko'lining janubidan Cleveland orqali boring Through Canada: NE to Midwest may cut across Ontario — without Canadian authority and passport, route south of Lake Erie through Cleveland
  • Paromlar: Long Island dan New England ga parom orqali yo'naltirishi mumkin — trucklar truck marshrutida NYC orqali haydashi kerak Ferries: Long Island to New England may route via ferry — trucks should drive through NYC on truck route
  • Lake Champlain: NY dan VT ga ko'l paromlari orqali yo'naltirishi mumkin — ko'prik orqali aylanib o'ting Lake Champlain: NY to VT may route via lake ferries — go around by bridge

Truck GPS yoki truck marshrutlash ilovasidan foydalaning. Google Maps truck balandligi, og'irligi yoki xavfli yuk cheklovlarini bilmaydi. Use truck GPS or truck routing app. Google Maps does NOT know truck height, weight, or hazmat limits.

Aylanma yo'llar (Bypasses) Bypasses

  • Katta shaharlar atrofidan o'tadigan yo'llar — trucklar shahar markazidan o'tmasligi uchun Roads going around big cities so trucks avoid downtown traffic
  • Halqa yo'llar = beltway — odatda 3 raqamli Interstate raqami (masalan, Atlanta atrofida I-285) Ring roads = beltways — usually 3-digit Interstate number (e.g., I-285 around Atlanta)

8.9 Avtomagistral qoidalari Highway Rules

Yo'l chetlari (Shoulders) Shoulders

  • Faqat favqulodda holat uchun — hech qachon dam olish yoki uxlash uchun to'xtamang For emergencies only — never stop to rest or sleep
  • Agar truck buzilsa: darhol xavf chiroqlari YOQILSIN; 10 daqiqa ichida 3 ta ogohlantirish uchburchagini qo'ying If truck breaks down: hazard lights ON immediately; within 10 minutes set 3 warning triangles
  • Ikki tomonlama yo'l: 10 fut orqada, 100 fut orqada, 100 fut oldinda Two-way road: 10 ft behind, 100 ft behind, 100 ft ahead
  • Bo'lingan yo'l: 10 fut, 100 fut, 200 fut orqada Divided highway: 10 ft, 100 ft, 200 ft behind

Qochish yo'lagi (Runaway Truck Ramp) Runaway Truck Ramp

  • Uzoq pastga yonbag'irda qochish yo'lagi — chuqur yumshoq shag'al tepaga qarab truckni tormozi buzilganda to'xtatadi Escape lane on long downhill grade — deep loose gravel going uphill stops truck with failed brakes
  • Oldini olish: Tepalikdan oldin past tezlik tanlang, bir xil xavfsiz tezlik, tormozlarni surishtirmang. Hech qachon rampaga kirishda to'xtamang Prevention: Pick low gear before hill, steady safe speed, don't ride brakes. Never park in ramp entrance

Qora muz (Black Ice) Black Ice

  • Yupqa, shaffof muz qatlami — yo'l ko'rinadi, haydovchilar ko'rmaydi Thin, clear ice layer — road shows through, drivers don't see it
  • Muzlash nuqtasi yaqinida hosil bo'ladi (32°F / 0°C), ko'pincha kechqurun/erta tongda Forms near freezing (32°F / 0°C), often at night/early morning
  • Ko'priklar, o'tkazgichlar, soyali joylar birinchi muzlaydi Bridges, overpasses, shaded spots freeze first
  • Ogohlantirish belgilari: ho'l yaltiroq yo'l, oynalardagi muz, boshqa shinalardan purkalish yo'q Warning signs: wet shiny road, ice on mirrors, no spray from other tires
  • Muzda: juda sekin yuring, xavfsiz bo'lganda haydashni to'xtating On ice: slow to crawl, stop driving when safe

Shina zanjirlari (Tire Chains) Tire Chains

  • Qor/muzda tutish uchun — yakkama, juft, kabel turlari For grip on snow/ice — single, double, cable types
  • Kolorado zanjir qonuni: 1-sentabr — 31-may, I-70 da Dotsero (mil 133) va Morrison (mil 259) orasida har bir tijorat transporti zanjir olib yurishi shart Colorado chain law: Sept 1 – May 31, every commercial vehicle on I-70 between Dotsero (mile 133) and Morrison (mile 259) must carry chains
  • Shuningdek: Kaliforniya, Oregon, Vashington, Aydaxo, Montana tog' dovonlarida zanjir nazorati qo'yadi Also: California, Oregon, Washington, Idaho, Montana post chain controls on mountain passes

Qurilish zonalari va truck taqiqlangan yo'llar Construction Zones and No-Truck Roads

  • Qurilish: qatorlar yopiladi, tezlik chegaralari tushadi, aylanma yo'llar; ko'p shtatlar ish zonalarida tezlik jazolarini ikki barobar oshiradi Construction: lanes close, speed limits drop, detours; many states double speeding fines in work zones
  • Truck taqiqlangan yo'llar: shaharlar parkwaylar va ko'chalarni trucklarga yopadi; oxirgi millarni truck marshrutlari, past ko'priklar, og'irlik chegaralari uchun tekshiring No-truck roads: cities close parkways and streets to trucks; check last miles for truck routes, low bridges, weight limits

8.10 Pullik yo'llar Toll Roads

Pullik yo'l (turnpike/tollway) to'lov oladi — trucklar ko'proq to'laydi (o'qlar bo'yicha, ba'zan og'irlik bo'yicha). Toll road (turnpike/tollway) charges fee — trucks pay more (by axles, sometimes weight).

E-ZPass

Bitta transponder ~20 shtat uchun (Sharq/Midwest, Meyndan Illinoysgacha, Floridagacha) One transponder for ~20 states (East/Midwest, Maine to Illinois to Florida)

Bestpass

Avtopark pullik yo'l dasturi — bitta hisob/qurilma butun mamlakat bo'ylab to'laydi, truck uchun bitta hisob-kitob Fleet toll program — one account/device pays nationwide, one bill per truck

PrePass

Vazn stansiyasini chetlab o'tish bilan tanilgan; PrePass Plus butun mamlakat bo'ylab pullik yo'lni qo'shadi Known for weigh-station bypass; PrePass Plus adds nationwide tolling

Elektron to'lov tizimi Electronic Tolling

  • Transponder: Yo'l ustidagi o'quvchi oynaga o'rnatilgan qurilma orqali hisobdan to'lov oladi Transponder: Reader over lane charges account via device on windshield
  • Plitali kamera: Qurilma yo'q → kamera plitani o'qiydi, egasiga yuqori narxda hisob yuboriladi Plate camera: No device → camera reads plate, bill to owner at higher rate
  • Naqdsiz yo'llar: Ko'plari endi naqd qabul qilmaydi Cashless roads: Many no longer take cash
  • Xarajat rejalashtirish: Pullik yo'l kalkulyatori (TollGuru) yoki truck GPS dan foydalaning Cost planning: Use toll calculator (TollGuru) or truck GPS

8.11 Tortish va tiklash Towing and Recovery

  • Tow truck buzilgan transportni ta'mirlash ustaxonasiga olib boradi Tow truck moves disabled vehicle to repair shop
  • Recovery = qiyinroq: halokat/ag'darilish/ariqqa tushgandan keyin truckni tortib chiqarish Recovery = harder: pulling truck back after crash/rollover/slide into ditch
  • Yuklangan semi uchun og'ir yuk tortgich (heavy-duty wrecker) kerak; ag'darilish uchun rotator (kranni tortish mashinasi) kerak bo'lishi mumkin Loaded semi needs heavy-duty wrecker; rollover may need rotator (crane tow truck)

Tayyor bo'ling: Have ready:

  • Aniq joylashuv — avtomagistral, yo'nalish, mil belgisi Exact location — highway, direction, mile marker
  • Avval tekshiring: tashuvchining yo'l yordami rejasi yoki sug'urtasi; tortishdan oldin narxini oling Check first: carrier's roadside plan or insurance; get price before tow

Asosiy fikrlar Key Takeaways

  • AQSh 4 ta asosiy mintaqaga bo'linadi (Shimoli-sharq, Midwest, Janub, G'arb) va 4 ta vaqt zonasiga ega. Dispetcher har doim uchrashuv vaqt zonasini tasdiqlashi kerak. The US is divided into 4 main regions (Northeast, Midwest, South, West) and has 4 time zones. Dispatchers must always confirm appointment time zones.
  • Interstate trucklar shtatlararo yuk tashiydi (21+ yosh, DOT tibbiy karta kerak), intrastate esa faqat bir shtat ichida. Interstate avtomagistrallarda toq raqamlar shimol-janubga, juft raqamlar sharq-g'arbga boradi. Interstate trucks haul across state lines (21+, DOT medical card required), intrastate within one state only. On interstates, odd numbers run north-south, even numbers east-west.
  • Marshrutlashda tog'lar, ob-havo va katta shahar tirbandliklari hisobga olinishi kerak. Avtomobil xaritalari (Google Maps) truck balandligi, og'irligi va xavfli yuk chegaralarini bilmaydi — truck GPS dan foydalaning. Routing must account for mountains, weather, and big city congestion. Car maps (Google Maps) don't know truck height, weight, or hazmat limits — use truck GPS.
  • Yo'l chetlari faqat favqulodda vaziyat uchun. Qora muz ko'rinmas xavf. Kolorado I-70 da zanjir qonuni mavjud (sentabr-may). Qurilish zonalarida jarima ikki barobar oshirilishi mumkin. Shoulders are for emergencies only. Black ice is an invisible hazard. Colorado has a chain law on I-70 (Sept–May). Construction zone fines may double.
  • Pullik yo'llar uchun E-ZPass (~20 shtat), Bestpass (butun mamlakat, bitta hisob) va PrePass (vazn stansiyasi bypass + pullik yo'l) mavjud. Ko'p yo'llar endi faqat elektron to'lov qabul qiladi. For toll roads: E-ZPass (~20 states), Bestpass (nationwide, one bill), and PrePass (weigh station bypass + tolling). Many roads now accept only electronic payment.
