Foydali resurslar Useful Resources

Dispatcher va logistika sohasida bilimingizni oshirish uchun eng yaxshi ilovalar, kanallar va manbalar. The best apps, channels and sources to deepen your knowledge in dispatching and logistics.

Ilovalar (Apps) Apps
DAT One
AQShdagi eng katta yuk va transport bozori. Yuklar qidirish, narxlarni solishtirish va brokerlar bilan bog'lanish uchun. The largest freight marketplace in the US. Search loads, compare rates, and connect with brokers.
Paid
Trucker Path
Haydovchilar uchun to'xtash joylari, yoqilg'i narxlari, tarozi stansiyalari va boshqa ma'lumotlar xaritasi. Map of truck stops, fuel prices, weigh stations, and other useful info for drivers.
Free
Motive (Keeptruckin)
ELD (elektron jurnal), GPS kuzatuv, haydovchilar xavfsizligi va avtopark boshqaruvi platformasi. ELD, GPS tracking, driver safety, and fleet management platform.
Paid
Sylectus
Dispetcherlar uchun TMS (Transport Boshqaruv Tizimi). Yuklar, haydovchilar va hisob-kitoblarni bir joyda boshqarish. TMS (Transportation Management System) for dispatchers. Manage loads, drivers and accounting in one place.
Paid
Truckstop
Yuk qidirish, narxlarni tahlil qilish va carrier/broker tekshirish uchun platforma. Load board, rate analytics, and carrier/broker vetting platform.
Paid
Google Maps
Marshrutlarni rejalashtirish, masofani hisoblash va real vaqt traffic holati. Dispetcherlar har kuni ishlatadi. Route planning, distance calculation and real-time traffic. Used daily by dispatchers.
Free
YouTube kanallari YouTube Channels
Smart Trucking
Yuk tashish sanoati haqida keng qamrovli videolar — yangi haydovchilar va dispetcherlar uchun. Comprehensive videos about the trucking industry — for new drivers and dispatchers.
Trucker Josh VLOGS
Haydovchilik hayoti, CDL maslahatlar va yuk tashish yangiliklari. Trucking life, CDL tips, and freight industry news.
Freight Broker Boot Camp
Freight broker va dispetcherlar uchun o'quv videolar — yuklar, muzokaralar, bozor tahlili. Training videos for freight brokers and dispatchers — loads, negotiations, market analysis.
The Load Board
Yuk tashish sanoati yangiliklari, tahlillar va interview'lar. Trucking industry news, analysis, and interviews.
Driving Academy
CDL imtihoniga tayyorgarlik, haydovchilik ko'nikmalari va yuk mashinalari haqida o'quv videolar. CDL exam prep, driving skills, and trucking educational videos.
Big Rig Steve
OTR haydovchilik tajribasi, marshrutlar va yuk tashish sanoati haqida real vloglar. Real vlogs about OTR driving experience, routes, and the trucking industry.
Reddit va hamjamiyatlar Reddit & Communities
r/Truckers
AQSh haydovchilari hamjamiyati — tajriba almashish, maslahatlar, yangiliklar. US truckers community — sharing experiences, advice, and news.
r/FreightBrokers
Freight brokerlar va dispetcherlar uchun muhokamalar — narxlar, mijozlar, texnologiya. Discussions for freight brokers and dispatchers — rates, clients, technology.
r/truckdispatchers
Dispetcherlar uchun maxsus subreddit — ish qidirish, TMS maslahatlar, brokerlar bilan munosabat. Dedicated subreddit for dispatchers — job search, TMS tips, broker relations.
TheTruckersReport Forum
AQShdagi eng katta yuk tashish forumi — CDL, kompaniyalar sharhlari, maslahatlar. The largest trucking forum in the US — CDL, company reviews, tips.
Davlat saytlari Government Sites
FMCSA
Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration — yuk tashish xavfsizligi qoidalari, litsenziyalar, HOS qoidalari. Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration — trucking safety regulations, licenses, HOS rules.
Gov
SAFER (FMCSA)
Carrier va broker ma'lumotlarini tekshirish — MC/DOT raqami, sug'urta, xavfsizlik reytingi. Look up carrier and broker info — MC/DOT number, insurance, safety rating.
Gov
FMCSA Clearinghouse
Haydovchilarning giyohvand moddalar testi natijalari bazasi — ishga olishdan oldin tekshirish majburiy. Drug and alcohol testing results database for drivers — pre-employment checks required.
Gov
FMCSA Licensing & Insurance
Carrier litsenziyasi va sug'urta ma'lumotlarini onlayn tekshirish. Check carrier licensing and insurance information online.
Gov
US DOT
AQSh Transport Departamenti — federal transport siyosati va qoidalari. US Department of Transportation — federal transportation policy and regulations.
Gov
Kitoblar va podkastlar Books & Podcasts
📖Erta Fletcher — "Freight Broker Business Startup"
Freight broker va dispatcher biznesini noldan boshlash uchun amaliy qo'llanma. Amazon'da mavjud. Practical guide to starting a freight broker and dispatcher business from scratch. Available on Amazon.
📖 Timothy Brady — "How to Become a Freight Broker"
Freight broker litsenziyasi olish, mijozlar topish va operatsiyalarni boshqarish bo'yicha kitob. Book on getting a freight broker license, finding clients, and managing operations.
🎧 FreightWaves Podcasts
Yuk tashish sanoati yangiliklari, bozor tahlili va ekspert intervyu'lari. Bir nechta podkast seriyalari. Trucking industry news, market analysis, and expert interviews. Multiple podcast series.
