AQSh xavfsizlik va muvofiqlik US Safety and Compliance

47 slayd  ·  DOT, FMCSA, CSA, haydovchi malakasi, ruxsatnomalar, sug'urta, tekshiruvlar va qoidabuzarliklar 47 slides  ·  DOT, FMCSA, CSA, driver qualification, permits, insurance, inspections and violations

9.1 Nima uchun xavfsizlik muhim Why Safety Matters

AQShda har yili 5,000 dan ortiq odam og'ir yuk mashinalari bilan bog'liq halokatlarda halok bo'ladi Over 5,000 people die annually in the US in crashes involving large trucks

Xavfsizlik bo'limi tashuvchi kompaniyaning eng muhim qismlaridan biri. U haydovchilarni himoya qiladi va kompaniyaning muvofiqligini ta'minlaydi. The safety department is one of the most critical parts of a carrier. It protects drivers and ensures the company stays compliant.

Xavfsizlik bo'limi nimalar ustida ishlaydi: What the safety department works on:

  • Halokatlar sonini kamaytirish Lowering crash numbers
  • Texnik xizmat standartlarini saqlash Maintaining maintenance standards
  • HOS (xizmat soatlari) muvofiqligini ta'minlash Ensuring HOS (Hours of Service) compliance
  • Hujjatlarning to'g'ri va yangilangan bo'lishini ta'minlash Keeping documents correct and up to date

9.2 Taniqli yuk mashinasi halokatlari Notable Truck Accidents

I-95, Baltimore (2016)

Yuk mashinasi to'xtagan transport ustiga haydab ketdi, ko'p transportli zanjirli halokat yuz berdi Truck drove into stopped traffic, causing a multi-vehicle chain-reaction crash

I-94, Kalamazoo (2015)

Qor bo'ronida bir nechta yuk mashinalari va yengil avtomobillar qatnashshgan ommaviy halokat Massive pileup involving multiple trucks and cars in a snowstorm

I-5, Sacramento

Jiddiy yuk mashinasi halokati — xavfsizlik tartib-qoidalariga rioya qilish zarurligi haqida ogohlantirish Serious truck crash — a reminder of the importance of following safety regulations

9.3 Yuk tashishni kim tartibga soladi Who Regulates Trucking

DOT (Department of Transportation)

Federal idora — barcha transport turlarini nazorat qiladi (avtomobil, temir yo'l, havo, dengiz) Federal agency — oversees all transportation modes (road, rail, air, sea)

FMCSA (Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration)

DOT tarkibida — faqat tijorat yuk mashinalari va avtobuslar xavfsizligi uchun mas'ul Under DOT — responsible only for commercial truck and bus safety

49 CFR — Federal qoidalar 49 CFR — Federal Regulations

Yuk tashish qoidalari 49 CFR (Code of Federal Regulations) da joylashgan: Parts 300–399 (yuk tashish) va Parts 100–180 (xavfli materiallar). Trucking regulations are in 49 CFR (Code of Federal Regulations): Parts 300–399 (trucking) and Parts 100–180 (hazardous materials).

9.5 Xavfsizlik ballari — SMS va CSA Safety Scores — SMS and CSA

SMS (Safety Measurement System) SMS (Safety Measurement System)

  • FMCSA ning 24 oylik xavfsizlik tarixini o'lchaydigan tizimi FMCSA's system that measures 24 months of safety history
  • 7 ta BASIC (Behavior Analysis and Safety Improvement Categories) kategoriyasini baholaydi Evaluates 7 BASICs (Behavior Analysis and Safety Improvement Categories)

9.8 Haydovchi malakasi (DQ fayl) Driver Qualification (DQ File)

Tajriba va kelib chiqish Experience and Background

  • Aksariyat tashuvchilar 1-2 yillik CDL tajribasini talab qiladi Most carriers require 1-2 years CDL experience
  • MVR (Motor Vehicle Record): Haydovchilik tarixini tekshirish MVR (Motor Vehicle Record): Check driving history
  • PSP (Pre-Employment Screening Program): FMCSA dan tekshiruv va halokat tarixini olish PSP (Pre-Employment Screening Program): Get inspection and crash history from FMCSA

DOT tibbiy tekshiruvi DOT Medical Exam

  • Sertifikatlangan DOT tibbiy tekshiruvchisi tomonidan o'tkaziladi Performed by a certified DOT medical examiner
  • Tibbiy karta 24 oy amal qiladi (ba'zi holatlar qisqaroq) Medical card valid for 24 months (shorter for some conditions)

Giyohvand moddalar va alkogol testi Drug and Alcohol Testing

  • Qachon test qilinadi: Ishga olishdan oldin, tasodifiy, halokat keyin, asosli shubha, qaytish testi When tested: Pre-employment, random, post-accident, reasonable suspicion, return-to-duty
  • 5 ta modda: Marihuana, kokain, amfetaminlar, opioidlar, PCP 5 drugs: Marijuana, cocaine, amphetamines, opioids, PCP
  • FMCSA Clearinghouse: Barcha ijobiy testlar va rad etishlar markaziy bazada saqlanadi — ish beruvchi oldingi buzilishlarni ko'radi FMCSA Clearinghouse: All positive tests and refusals stored in central database — employers can see prior violations

Jinoyat va kredit tarixi Criminal and Credit History

  • FCRA (Fair Credit Reporting Act) qoidalariga muvofiq o'tkaziladi Conducted under FCRA (Fair Credit Reporting Act) rules
  • Muhim: Odamlarni qidirish veb-saytlaridan foydalanmang — faqat rasmiy xizmatlar orqali Important: Do not use people-search websites — use only official services

9.14 Halokatdan keyingi qadamlar Post-Accident Steps

5 ta asosiy qadam 5 Key Steps

  • 1. Xavfsizlikni ta'minlang — xavf chiroqlarini yoqing, ogohlantirish uchburchaklarini qo'ying 1. Secure the scene — hazard lights on, set warning triangles
  • 2. 911 ga qo'ng'iroq qiling (zarar yetganlarga yordam, politsiya hisoboti) 2. Call 911 (help injured, get police report)
  • 3. Kompaniyaga (dispetcher/xavfsizlik bo'limiga) xabar bering 3. Notify company (dispatcher/safety department)
  • 4. Hujjatlashtiring — suratlar, boshqa haydovchi ma'lumotlari, guvohlar, politsiya hisoboti raqami 4. Document — photos, other driver info, witnesses, police report number
  • 5. Hech narsani tan olmang — faqat faktlarni ayting 5. Don't admit fault — state only facts

Giyohvand moddalar/alkogol testi qoidalari Drug/Alcohol Test Rules

  • Har doim test qilinadi: Agar halokat natijasida biror kishi halok bo'lsa Always tested: If someone dies as a result of the accident
  • Boshqa hollarda test: Agar truck haydovchisiga jarima yozilsa VA tibbiy yordam ko'rsatilsa YOKI transport vositasi olib ketilsa Otherwise tested: If the truck driver gets a citation AND someone receives medical treatment OR a vehicle is towed

Asosiy fikrlar Key Takeaways

  • AQShda yiliga 5,000+ odam truck halokatlarida halok bo'ladi. DOT va FMCSA yuk tashishni tartibga soladi; asosiy qoidalar 49 CFR da, jumladan 382 (giyohvand test), 391 (haydovchi malakasi), 395 (HOS) qismlarida. Over 5,000 die annually in US truck crashes. DOT and FMCSA regulate trucking; key rules are in 49 CFR, including Parts 382 (drug testing), 391 (driver qualification), and 395 (HOS).
  • Har bir tashuvchiga USDOT raqami kerak; shtatlararo yollanma tashuvchilarga MC raqami ham kerak. FMCSA SAFER Company Snapshot (safer.fmcsa.dot.gov) har qanday tashuvchi haqida ma'lumot beradi. Every carrier needs a USDOT number; interstate for-hire carriers also need an MC number. FMCSA SAFER Company Snapshot (safer.fmcsa.dot.gov) provides data on any carrier.
  • CSA/SMS tizimi 7 ta BASIC kategoriyada 24 oylik xavfsizlik tarixini o'lchaydi. Foiz ko'rsatkich 65% dan oshsa (halokat uchun 80%), FMCSA aralashishi mumkin. Yangi tashuvchilar 12 oy ichida auditdan o'tadi. CSA/SMS measures 24 months of safety history across 7 BASICs. Percentiles above 65% (80% for crashes) trigger FMCSA intervention. New carriers are audited within 12 months.
  • Haydovchi DQ faylida CDL tajribasi, MVR, PSP, DOT tibbiy karta (24 oy), giyohvand test natijalari (Clearinghouse) va FCRA talablariga muvofiq jinoyat/kredit tekshiruvi bo'lishi kerak. Driver DQ file must include CDL experience, MVR, PSP, DOT medical card (24 months), drug test results (Clearinghouse), and criminal/credit check per FCRA requirements.
  • CDL endorsementlari: H (hazmat), N (tanker), T (ikki tirkegli), X (H+N). IFTA har chorakda yoqilg'i solig'i hisoboti talab qiladi. 5 ta shtat og'irlik-masofa solig'i oladi. Kaliforniya CARB qoidalari qat'iy. CDL endorsements: H (hazmat), N (tanker), T (doubles/triples), X (H+N). IFTA requires quarterly fuel tax reporting. 5 states charge weight-distance tax. California CARB rules are strict.
  • 7 xil sug'urta turi mavjud. Auto Liability (min $750K) majburiy. Brokerlar odatda $1M auto, $100K cargo va GL talab qiladi. DOT tekshiruvlari I dan VIII gacha darajada o'tkaziladi. There are 7 insurance types. Auto Liability (min $750K) is required. Brokers typically require $1M auto, $100K cargo, and GL. DOT inspections run from Level I to VIII.
  • Halokatdan keyin: joy xavfsizligini ta'minlang, 911 ga qo'ng'iroq qiling, kompaniyaga xabar bering, hujjatlashtiring, aybni tan olmang. O'lim yuz bersa giyohvand testi majburiy; boshqa hollarda jarima + tibbiy yordam/tortish bo'lsa test qilinadi. After an accident: secure the scene, call 911, notify company, document, don't admit fault. Drug test is mandatory if death occurs; otherwise test when citation + medical treatment or towing.
